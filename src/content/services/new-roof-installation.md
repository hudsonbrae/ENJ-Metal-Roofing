New construction roofing is installed to the engineer's and builder's
specification — profile, pitch, fixing pattern and flashing details are
usually set before work begins.

## Bushfire-prone land

Much of the Shoalhaven is mapped as bushfire prone. Where a Bushfire Attack
Level applies under AS 3959, the standard sets requirements for roof
construction — including sealing gaps at ridges and eaves against ember
entry, and the materials used for gutters and valleys.

Metal roof sheeting is non-combustible, which is one reason it is common on
bushfire-prone land. The BAL rating for a specific property is determined
through the planning process, not on site.

## Wind and fixing

Coastal sites carry higher wind loading than inland ones. Fixing patterns —
how many screws per sheet and where they land — follow the manufacturer's
specification for the relevant wind classification.
