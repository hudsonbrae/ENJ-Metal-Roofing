HOW TO ADD A PROJECT
════════════════════

1. Create a folder:   src/content/projects/<slug>/
2. Put the photos in that same folder.
3. Create index.md inside it, using the shape below.

The URL becomes /projects/<slug>/ automatically. The project appears in the
work index, the sitemap and the structured data with no other changes.

ONLY FILL IN WHAT IS ACTUALLY KNOWN. Every field below except title, status,
type and images is optional, and anything omitted simply does not render.
Do not invent a suburb, year, colour or scope to fill a gap — a project page
with fewer facts looks deliberate; a wrong fact damages trust.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---
title: Mollymook Beach Re-Roof
status: complete              # complete | in-progress
type: re-roof                 # re-roof | new-construction | repair |
                              # gutters-fascia | other
featured: true                # show on the homepage work index
order: 1                      # lower numbers sort first

# ── Everything below is optional — delete any line you cannot verify ──
suburb: Mollymook
state: NSW
year: 2026
roofProfile: Trimdek
material: COLORBOND steel
colour: Surfmist
scope:
  - Removal of existing tile roof
  - New sarking and insulation
  - New gutters and downpipes
summary: >
  Two sentences, factual, describing what the job involved.
  No superlatives, no claims about satisfaction or speed.

images:
  - src: ./hero.jpg
    alt: Completed Trimdek roof in Surfmist viewed from the street
    role: hero                # hero | wide | detail | before | during | after
  - src: ./seam-detail.jpg
    alt: Close view of ridge capping and sheet ends
    role: detail
    caption: Ridge capping and sheet ends
---

Optional longer description in markdown goes here. It is not required.
