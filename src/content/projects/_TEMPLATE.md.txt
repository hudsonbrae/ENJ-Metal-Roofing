HOW TO ADD A PROJECT
====================

1. Create a folder:   src/content/projects/<project-name>/
   Use lowercase words joined by hyphens, e.g. mollymook-re-roof.
   It becomes the page address: /projects/mollymook-re-roof/
2. Put that job's photos in the same folder (JPEG straight from the camera
   or phone is fine; the site resizes and converts them).
3. Create index.md in the folder, copying the shape below.

The project appears on the Work page, the homepage and the sitemap with no
other changes. Photos are optimised automatically.

ONLY FILL IN WHAT IS ACTUALLY KNOWN. Everything except `title` is optional,
and anything left out simply does not appear. Do not guess a suburb, date,
colour or product to fill a gap.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------

---
title: Mollymook re-roof
service: re-roofing        # re-roofing | new-roof-installation | roof-repairs | guttering-fascia
summary: One or two factual sentences about the job.
featured: true             # leads the homepage and the Work page
order: 1                   # lower numbers first

# Optional facts. Delete any line you cannot confirm.
location:
  suburb: Mollymook
  state: NSW
completed: 2026-09         # YYYY or YYYY-MM
roof:
  profile: Trimdek
  material: COLORBOND steel
  colour: Surfmist
scope:
  - Removed the existing tile roof
  - New sarking and insulation
  - New gutters and downpipes

# Photos. Every photo needs alt text describing what it shows.
hero:                      # the main photo; landscape works best
  src: ./front.jpg
  alt: Finished Surfmist roof seen from the street
gallery:
  - src: ./aerial.jpg
    alt: Drone view of the whole roof
    kind: wide             # wide | detail | during
  - src: ./ridge.jpg
    alt: Close-up of the ridge capping
    kind: detail           # detail photos also appear in the homepage "Where roofs fail" section
    caption: Ridge capping and sheet ends
  - src: ./install.jpg
    alt: New sheets going on
    kind: during
beforeAfter:
  - before:
      src: ./before.jpg
      alt: The old tile roof before work started
    after:
      src: ./after.jpg
      alt: The same view with the new metal roof
    caption: Taken from the same spot before and after
---

Optional longer description, in plain paragraphs. Keep it factual.
